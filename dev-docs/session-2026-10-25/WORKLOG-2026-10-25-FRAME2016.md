## Root cause isolated

The launcher and diagnostic wrapper included asteroid atlas `d1feb687` in
`TOUCHHLE_DS_DEPTH_LEQUAL_HASH`, with `TOUCHHLE_DS_DEPTH_LEQUAL_NOWRITE=1`.
When its projected bounds passed the screen-size gate, `texnodepth_begin`
changed this real 600-index mesh from GL_LESS/depth-write-on to
GL_LEQUAL/depth-write-off. Later triangles could overwrite its front surface.
This explains the rectangular missing rock and the false foreground rock
produced by earlier no-cull/GL_ALWAYS experiments.

Removing only d1feb687 from that background list restores the continuous rock.
The original asteroid culling can stay enabled. Foundation workaround
`02d3486c:405` is retained.
