### Black weapon

An early trace appeared to implicate `56c62e8a`, but its log crossed two process
runs and was invalid. Logs are now isolated per launch.

The clean same-PID trace at landscape point `(250,190)`, frame `12360`, found
the final responsible draw:

```text
kind=elements count=2532 tex=78 key=9bdc088c
before=[3,4,5,255] after=[0,0,0,253]
depthFunc=513 depth mask=1
blend=false
cull=true
```

The final pixel is touched by several material batches (`9bdc088c`,
`60bb1a47`, and room-atlas batches). `TOUCHHLE_DS_FOG_RED=1` changes the entire
black region to red, so the failure is fog saturation, not black texture data.

The live state was:

```text
GL_LINEAR fog
colour=[0,0,0,1]
start=-0.001
end=0
density=1
```

This degenerate range makes every positive eye-space distance resolve to the
black fog colour. The renderer now disables fog for a draw when linear fog has
`end <= start` or `end <= 0`, while preserving valid positive fog ranges.

A triangle-only skip was tested and is not sufficient because multiple material
batches are affected. Removing `56c62e8a:6` was also tested and is not valid;
it darkens the porthole room.

### Opening foundation rock culling

The missing station-foundation rock was reproduced with resolve-time captures.
At `f=10800`, the default build leaves the right half of the station foundation
transparent while the official Android capture has a continuous rock wall.

`TOUCHHLE_DS_UV_PROBE=370,120` identified these draw batches over the missing
point:

```text
02d3486c count=405  cull=true
d1feb687 count=600  cull=true
f4d178fa count=1083 cull=true
```

The rock atlas is `d1feb687`, but the draw that restores the missing surface is
the station batch `02d3486c/count=405`. Disabling culling only for that batch
restores the same continuous foundation geometry seen in the official capture.
The compatibility rule is content/count based and has no chapter or frame gate:

```text
02d3486c:405
```

Validation:

- official rock comparison:
  `logs/foundation-default-fix2-20261024-0110/official-vs-default-fix-rock.jpg`
- frame-numbered OBS capture:
  `logs/foundation-default-fix2-20261024-0110/foundation-default-fix-frames.mp4`
- `f=10800`: foundation rock complete
- `f=12480`: corridor, poster and character normal

The continuous frame dumper now reads the resolved drawable instead of the
multisample source FBO, so PPM diagnostics match the presented image.

### FRAME 2016 foreground asteroid depth

The later logo fly-through still revealed a rectangular hollow in the large
foreground asteroid. UV tracing at the corresponding guest shot identified:

```text
d1feb687 count=600 cull=true
f4d178fa count=1083 cull=true
0712153f count=11427 cull=true
```

The `d1feb687` atlas is the asteroid artwork. Disabling depth entirely restored
the texture but let later background layers overwrite the rock, which was a
regression. The compatibility fix instead forces `GL_ALWAYS` only for
`d1feb687/count=600` while leaving depth writes enabled:

```text
TOUCHHLE_DS_DEPTH_ALWAYS_HASH_COUNT=d1feb687:600
```

The launch configuration also keeps:

```text
TOUCHHLE_DS_NO_CULL_HASH_COUNT=02d3486c:405;d1feb687:600
```

Verification through the real launcher reached `10fps FRAME 2016`, the
porthole, the weapon close-up and the corridor. The asteroid cavity was filled,
the weapon retained its metal/emissive material, and black-frame detection found
no additional scene transition black segment.
